FAQs

Common Questions

Do I need council approval for a Freedom Series home?
It depends on your council and intended use. Relocatable homes used as secondary dwellings or on rural land often have simpler approval pathways than permanent structures. We can advise based on your specific location and situation.
What site preparation is required?
You'll need a level, accessible pad — typically stumps, piers or a concrete slab. The exact spec depends on your model and soil conditions. We provide a site prep guide after your deposit is confirmed.
How are Freedom homes delivered and installed?
We transport the home on a flat-bed truck and position it with a crane or hiab. The expandable sections unfold on site — the whole setup process typically takes a few hours. You just need good access for a truck and crane.
Can I customise the interior layout or finishes?
Yes — all models offer a range of finish packages and optional upgrades including kitchen, flooring, cladding, solar and deck options. See the Options page or ask us for a full options sheet.
Is a Freedom home suitable as a permanent residence?
Absolutely. The Freedom 30 and 40 are designed as full family homes with all the fixtures and insulation of a permanent dwelling. For NCC Class 1a certified granny flats in SEQ, see our Certified Series.
