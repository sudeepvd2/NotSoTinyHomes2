Where it works

Where a SiteBox can work

Check approvals and tie-down requirements for your site before you order. It is not sold as accommodation.

⛏️

Remote Site Offices

Mining, construction and agricultural sites where you need a secure, weatherproof office that can be relocated as the project moves.

🏗️

Construction Site Sheds

Site sheds and lunchrooms that can be repositioned between stages. Electrical connection by a licensed electrician.

🏪

Pop-up Retail & Events

Brand activations, market stalls, ticketing booths and temporary kiosks. Deploy at an event, fold up and move on.

🌾

Farm & Rural Storage

Secure on-property storage or equipment rooms. Check what your council requires.

Choose your version

Standard or Premium Upgraded

The main difference is how much wind each version is rated for. The Standard rating is below the wind classes normally used for houses in South East Queensland, so choose Premium Upgraded for tougher conditions.

Standard
From $4,500
Rated 89 to 102 km/h by the manufacturer
For sheltered or lower-wind sites. Whether it suits your site depends on your wind region, shielding and tie-down. Check with an engineer if you are unsure.
Premium Upgraded
$6,500
N3+ and 200+ km/h wind-rated models
For tougher conditions. The wind class for your site and the tie-down are confirmed by an engineer. Ask us for the full Premium specification.
