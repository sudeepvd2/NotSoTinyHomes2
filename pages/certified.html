FAQ

Common questions

Do I need council approval?+
You need building approval, and in many cases planning approval as well, depending on your zoning and overlays. Your building certifier and council can confirm this for your block. Granny flat rules differ between councils.
Is this a certified home?+
Not as a pre-approved product. Engineering and energy certificates are issued for a particular design and address. A certificate from one site cannot be reused on another.
Can I get a home loan?+
That is up to your lender. Ask early, because some lenders have rules for imported or factory-built homes. We can supply specifications and documents for your application.
Can I use my own builder?+
Yes, and you will need one who holds a suitable QBCC licence. We will give them the drawings and installation information we have.
What is the warranty?+
Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. Any additional warranty will be stated in your contract. Your builder's work is covered by their own obligations.
Will it pay for itself or add value?+
We cannot promise that. Rental return and property value depend on your location, approvals and market. Get independent advice.
