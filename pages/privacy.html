Privacy

Kiteworks Pty Ltd, trading as Not So Tiny Homes, collects the details you send through our enquiry form or by email and phone. We use them to answer your enquiry, prepare quotes and manage your order.

The enquiry form is delivered by Formspree, a third-party form service, which may store your message outside Australia. If you order, we share the details needed to deliver your order with people such as freight companies and crane operators.

We do not sell your details. To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email info@notsotinyhomes.com.au.
