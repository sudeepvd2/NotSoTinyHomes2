Common questions
Will it be approved on my block?+
The home is engineered and documented for building approval, but every lot is different. Lot size, setbacks, sewer location and council rules all matter. Send us your address and we'll check before you spend anything.
Can I get finance?+
It's a permanent, certified dwelling on titled land, but lending rules vary. Talk to your lender or mortgage broker about construction finance early — we can provide the documents they ask for.
What does the builder do?+
A QBCC-licensed builder prepares the site, builds the footings, places and ties down the home, arranges the electrician and plumber to connect services, takes out home warranty insurance and manages inspections through to final sign-off.
Can I use my own builder?+
Yes. We'll give your builder the engineering, drawings and specifications. Or we can introduce our partner builder.
How long does it take?+
Allow around 5–7 months: approvals first, then 40–50 days production, about 35 days sea freight, port clearance and installation.
