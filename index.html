What is a relocatable expandable home?

An expandable home is a prefabricated steel-frame building that folds for transport and opens out on site. It arrives pre-wired, pre-plumbed and fitted out, ready for licensed trades to connect.

Freedom Series homes are not documented as Class 1a dwellings. If you will live in one, rent it out or connect it to services, your council is likely to require building and plumbing approval, and possibly planning approval. Ask your council or a private certifier first. Fact sheets from councils such as Scenic Rim and Livingstone explain how use affects approvals.

Common questions

What is the difference between Freedom and Class 1a homes?+
Freedom Series homes are made to a standard specification and are not documented for Class 1a approval. Our 30ft Class 1a home has a layout and specification designed to meet those requirements, with engineering and energy assessment arranged for your project. Your certifier decides whether it can be approved on your site.
Do I need council approval for a Freedom Series home?+
Possibly. If you will live in it, rent it out or connect it to services, the council fact sheets we have read treat it as a dwelling, which needs building and plumbing approval and may need planning approval. Some councils exempt a registered caravan used only while travelling and not connected to services, but Freedom homes are delivered by truck and crane, so this will not normally fit. Ask your council before you order.
Where are the homes made?+
In China, by manufacturers we work with. Homes ship from Tianjin to Brisbane in about 40 days, and we handle customs and biosecurity clearance.
How long does delivery take?+
For a Freedom Series home, allow about 3 months from deposit. For a Class 1a home, allow about 14 to 16 weeks from deposit to delivery, after design and approval work is done. Factory schedules and shipping can change these times.
Can I customise the layout, colours and finishes?+
Yes. Choose from the finishes list and confirm colours against physical samples. Layout changes are quoted separately.
Is plumbing and electrical certified?+
A licensed Australian electrician and plumber must inspect and connect the home on site, and they are responsible for compliance of that work. We can provide supplier product certificates for nominated components. Ask us which apply to your order.
What warranty do I get?+
Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. Any additional warranty will be stated in your contract.
What's included in the Class 1a price?+
The indicative base price covers the fitted home, sea freight, customs and port charges. Delivery, crane, site preparation, builder work, connections and council fees are extra. See the cost breakdown. You use a QBCC-licensed builder for site work.
Do you have off-grid options?+
Tell us about your site. Solar, tank water and septic systems are arranged separately, need licensed trades and may need approvals.
What does delivery cost?+
We quote delivery by postcode and site access, because costs vary a lot. You also need a crane on delivery day, at least 3.5 tonne for a 20ft home.
Is finance available?+
We do not provide finance or credit advice. You can apply for a personal loan through an independent provider such as Amala Finance. Read its terms and compare other lenders. For a Class 1a home, ask your lender or broker about building finance early.
