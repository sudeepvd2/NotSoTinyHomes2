What is a relocatable expandable home?

An expandable home is a prefabricated steel-frame dwelling that folds flat for transport and expands on site to its full footprint. It arrives ready to connect — pre-wired, pre-plumbed and fully fitted out.

Because it travels on a registered transport vehicle, it is classified as a relocatable dwelling — which means in most rural and acreage zones no council building approval is required. Just place it, connect your services and move in.

Common questions

What is the difference between Freedom and Certified Series?+
Freedom Series homes are relocatable — placed on a trailer they require no council development approval in most cases. Certified Series homes are fully NCC Class 1a compliant permanent dwellings with council approval, home loan finance eligibility and full engineering certification.
Do I need council approval for a Freedom Series home?+
Generally no — when placed on a registered trailer, Freedom Series homes are classified as relocatable dwellings and do not require development approval in most QLD councils. For permanent placement with full council approval, choose the Certified Series.
How long does delivery take?+
For units in stock, typically 4–6 weeks. For new orders, production is 8–14 weeks plus 5 weeks shipping. Certified Series homes require an additional 4–8 weeks for engineering and certification before production.
Can I customise the layout, colours and finishes?+
Yes — all models are built to order and fully customisable. Choose your panel colour, frame colour, flooring, kitchen layout and bedroom configuration. Custom layouts are also accepted.
Is plumbing and electrical certified?+
Yes — all units come pre-fitted with WaterMark certified plumbing and SAA certified electrical components. A licensed Australian electrician and plumber must connect services on arrival.
Do you have off-grid options?+
Yes — we can configure homes for off-grid sites with solar and battery, generator backup, rainwater tank and septic systems. Contact us to discuss your requirements.
What does delivery cost?+
Delivery is quoted by postcode and site access. Typical costs are $800–$2,500 within Queensland. A crane (minimum 3.5 tonne) is required on delivery day.
